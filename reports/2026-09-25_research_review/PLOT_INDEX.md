# Plot index

Each metric has one comparison of the five methods and five figures showing individual seed curves, one per method. Comparison bands are ±1 sample SD across completed training seeds, not confidence intervals. MAPDN means use only seeds 1100–1102; seed 1103 is dashed in individual curves and seed 1104 is labeled missing. No smoothing or extrapolation.
