# Capability generalization and recovery: evidence audit

Research date: 25 September 2026. These notes distinguish inspected published results, recent preprints, and proposed implications. PDFs and download provenance are recorded in `../papers/manifest_capabilities.json`. This is a scoped review, not a systematic-review completeness claim.

**The closest baselines go beyond ROMA.** Learned roles, known-capability generalization, hidden-capability inference, and within-episode open teaming each already have primary precedents. A contribution must establish something about their particular combination and its measured recovery mechanism.

The original Guide 3 checked PL-MARL only at abstract level. This audit acquired its full text and located the node-failure experiment. The full-text files are for local research use, not evidence that every experiment or implementation has been reproduced.

**Proposed distinction.** Study a jointly trained, parameter-shared population with a fixed observation/action/capability schema. Agents have unknown, changing capability values. Their recurrent state estimates effective capability from execution-visible outcomes, while a separate soft gate chooses current computational responsibility from capability, demand, and communicated team context. Compare this against the same recurrent encoder and capability estimator with ordinary graph attention and with a CASH-style conditional decoder. A measured advantage after combined membership/capability events would support the specific routing mechanism. This remains a candidate contribution, because CE-CM and HOLA cover major adjacent claims.

**Information matching is decisive.** Giving SoftRole a capability label while denying it to the recurrent baseline tests information advantage. Giving CASH true current capability while SoftRole must infer it tests a different advantage in the opposite direction. Use an announced-capability panel and a hidden-capability panel. In the latter, give all architectural conditions the same estimator family, local outcome cues, history length, and packet budget. A true-capability policy is a diagnostic reference, not an upper bound on all trained policies.

**What performance reveals.** Recent local success is an outcome jointly affected by capability, opportunity, geometry, partner actions, task difficulty, and noise. A good capability estimator predicts action-conditioned outcomes, with uncertainty, rather than interpreting low reward as low capability. Do not feed a centralized critic's value, a counterfactual advantage, or an individual success label that the execution environment does not expose into the actor.

**What 'slightly different capabilities' should mean.** Use a fixed vector such as maximum speed, sensing reliability, sensing radius, and payload. Train on a declared region of this vector space. Hold out interpolation combinations first, then thin shells outside the region. New sensors with different observation semantics or entirely new actions are schema transfer, not a small capability perturbation. A finite bank of role experts cannot manufacture the missing interface.

**Useful mechanism split.** Event → locally detectable evidence → capability-belief update → role redistribution → useful information delivery → successful response. Log a timestamp and task outcome for each link. This distinguishes undetected failure, slow adaptation, missing backup information, and failure to act after receipt. Latent role classification and attention maps alone do not establish any link.
